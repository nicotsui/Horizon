### [Middle East Chemicals Retain Cost Edge Amid Hormuz Crisis](https://oilprice.com/Energy/Natural-Gas/Middle-East-Chemicals-Keep-Their-Cost-Edge-Despite-Hormuz-Crisis.html) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Middle East chemical producers retain a cost advantage despite the Iran war and Hormuz crisis, keeping polyethylene prices high. Global polyethylene prices have surged by more than 50% since March, while US manufacturers like Dow Chemical reported a 27% year-over-year increase in packaging segment sales. Buyers prioritize the Middle East&\#x27;s low prices over supply risks, ensuring the region retains its export dominance.

rss · OilPrice · Oct 17, 21:00

**「Background」** The Iran war began in late February 2026, constraining supply through the Strait of Hormuz and prompting diversification efforts. The Middle East remains the world&\#x27;s largest polyethylene exporter, accounting for 43% of global exports \(18.7 million tons in 2025\). US producers are filling the gap due to secure feedstocks, contrasting with high energy costs affecting Asian and European manufacturers.

**「Impact」** US chemical companies are the immediate winners, with Dow Chemical reporting a 27% year-over-year increase in packaging segment sales driven by higher polyethylene pricing.
