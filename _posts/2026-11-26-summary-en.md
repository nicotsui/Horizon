This is due to the Frontier having 16 times the memory capacity of Summit. Additionally, problematic GPU firmware and interconnect faults can cause severe issues. A single GPU failure can force a computation task using 1,024 GPUs to halt. GPUs with performance issues also pose challenges, as slower units can cause others to wait. Supercomputers use checkpointing mechanisms to save computation results at regular intervals, allowing simulation tasks to resume without starting over if an error occurs.

**「Impact」** The impact of these hardware issues on developers and organizations is significant, as they can lead to the gradual failure of large-scale simulations running on thousands of processors. This not only affects the efficiency of the supercomputer but also requires careful management of computation progress and potential rework.
