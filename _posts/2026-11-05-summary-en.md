**「Background on Embodied AI and NeuroMechFly」** Embodied AI, a field within artificial intelligence, focuses on creating intelligent systems that have a physical body and can interact with the environment. This approach is inspired by biological systems and aims to create AI that can learn and adapt through physical interaction. NeuroMechFly is a neuromechanical modeling platform that simulates embodied sensorimotor control in Drosophila, or fruit flies. It allows researchers to build models that integrate brain-level sensory integration and decision-making with VNC-level motor control, facilitating the study of how neural systems control movement.

**「Impact」** This research could significantly impact the development of embodied AI by providing a new approach to learning adaptability, potentially leading to more robust and versatile AI systems capable of handling diverse environments and tasks.
