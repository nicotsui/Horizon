### [Llmspeed: Benchmark local LLM performance before buying hardware](https://llmspeed.dev/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Llmspeed is a new web-based tool that allows users to benchmark local Large Language Model \(LLM\) inference speeds on their specific hardware configurations. The platform enables testing various model quantizations and sizes against a user&\#x27;s GPU and RAM to estimate tokens per second \(TPS\) before purchasing new equipment. It is currently available as a free web service accessible via llmspeed.dev.

hackernews · albinstman · Oct 17, 04:36 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50127979)

**「Background」** Evaluating the performance of local Large Language Models often requires downloading and running models on specific hardware, which is time-consuming and resource-intensive. Users frequently struggle to predict whether upgrading their GPU or RAM will yield a meaningful performance increase for their intended workloads.

**「Impact」** This tool enables developers to make more informed hardware purchasing decisions by providing concrete tokens-per-second benchmarks for their specific setups, potentially saving thousands of dollars on unnecessary upgrades.

**「Community Discussion」** Users debate the gap between synthetic benchmarks and real-world usage, noting that factors like parameter tuning and cache-aware execution often reduce throughput compared to idealized tests. Several commenters emphasize that single-chat benchmarks do not reflect performance drops when handling multiple parallel tasks or frequent context switches.
