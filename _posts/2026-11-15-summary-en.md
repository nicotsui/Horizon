It is the percentage of foreign exchange deposits that financial institutions must hold in reserve, rather than lend out or use for other purposes. This ratio has been adjusted by the People&\#x27;s Bank of China to promote balance in the foreign exchange market and manage currency expectations. The adjustment reflects a significant policy change that could impact the foreign exchange market and currency expectations, making it an important factor for readers tracking China&\#x27;s economy and stock market.

**「Impact」** The increase in the reserve requirement ratio may lead to a reduction in the liquidity available for foreign exchange transactions, potentially affecting currency exchange rates and market expectations.
