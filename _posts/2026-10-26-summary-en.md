**「Market Dynamics and AI Impact」** The analysis of AI&\#x27;s impact on the stock market and economy has shown that the slowdown in AI trends may not necessarily indicate a bear market. This is due to the unique characteristics of the AI industry, which has not historically had a broad positive effect on other economic sectors like previous trends such as industrial and financial in 2006-2007, real estate and infrastructure in 2009, mobile internet in 2013-2015, and energy transformation in 2019-2021. The AI industry has, in fact, had a significant negative impact on other sectors, including rising costs of materials and resources, increased debt financing for infrastructure, and job displacement. This suggests that the traditional relationship between industry trends and market cycles may not apply to the current AI-driven market. Additionally, the analysis indicates that a slowdown in AI investment could potentially benefit non-AI sectors by improving macroeconomic conditions such as interest rates.

**「Impact」** The slowdown in AI investment could lead to a more favorable environment for non-AI sectors. This is due to the AI industry&\#x27;s negative externalities, such as increased costs and job displacement, which could ease if AI investment slows down. Non-AI sectors may benefit from a reduction in these negative effects and potentially from lower interest rates.
