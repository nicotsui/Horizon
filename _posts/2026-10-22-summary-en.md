### [China&\#x27;s Policy Rate Anchor Shift: Impact Analysis](https://wallstreetcn.com/member/articles/3784118) ⭐️ 9.0/10

China&\#x27;s central bank announced a significant policy shift by switching the main operation variety of open market operations from 7-day to overnight, aligning policy rates with market benchmarks. This move, effective from October 19, 2026, marks a transition from quantity-based to price-based monetary control, with the short-end market rate as the core. The overnight repo rate \(DR001\) will now serve as the primary policy rate, potentially lowering the interest rate anchor by 15 basis points compared to the current 7-day reverse repurchase rate.

rss · 华尔街见闻 · Oct 22, 00:12

**「Background on China&\#x27;s Policy Rate Shift」** The People&\#x27;s Bank of China \(PBOC\) has historically employed a quantity-based monetary policy framework, focusing on controlling the money supply. This approach has been characterized by the use of various tools, including open market operations with a 7-day maturity as the primary operation variety. However, this framework has been subject to criticism for not fully aligning with market dynamics and for potentially causing distortions in financial markets. The shift to an overnight operation variety and the adoption of the DR001 as the main policy rate represents a move towards a price-based monetary policy framework, which is more aligned with market benchmarks and is expected to enhance the effectiveness of monetary policy transmission. This change is part of a broader effort to deepen interest rate liberalization and improve the efficiency of the financial system.

**「Impact」** This policy change is expected to have a concrete impact on the A-share market and the broader economy. The shift to a short-end market rate as the primary policy rate may lead to lower borrowing costs for companies, potentially boosting investment and economic growth. However, the actual extent of the easing effect will depend on whether the new policy rate leads to a decrease in short-term interest rates and improves liquidity conditions.
