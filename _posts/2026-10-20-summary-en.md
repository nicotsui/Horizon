These changes are expected to have a significant impact on the market.

rss · 华尔街见闻 · Oct 20, 12:03

**「Background on China&\#x27;s Monetary Policy Framework」** China&\#x27;s monetary policy framework has evolved significantly over the years. The central bank has transitioned from a focus on quantity-based controls to a more price-based approach. This shift has been marked by changes in policy interest rate anchors and loan interest rate benchmarks. The introduction of the 7-day reverse repurchase agreement as the main policy interest rate in 2024 was a step towards this price-based framework. Additionally, the gradual phasing out of other term tools and the introduction of more diversified loan interest rate pricing benchmarks reflect this shift. The recent announcement of further reforms, including the potential switch to overnight operations and the diversification of loan interest rate benchmarks, is part of this ongoing transformation. This evolution is in line with international practices and aims to enhance the precision of liquidity management and the effectiveness of monetary policy transmission mechanisms.

**「Impact on Investors and Market」** The shift to overnight policy rate and the diversification of loan interest rate benchmarks are expected to enhance the precision of liquidity management and improve the transmission mechanism of policy rates to market rates. This could lead to clearer price signals in the financial market and the real economy, potentially benefiting investors who can adapt to the new pricing benchmarks and liquidity management strategies.
